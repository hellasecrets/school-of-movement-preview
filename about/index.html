Frequently Asked Questions

A little more about moving with us.

Do I need experience to join a class?

Not at all! Whether you're new to movement or have been practicing for years, School of Movement is about finding what works for you and building confidence along the way.

What's the difference between Pilates, mobility, and assisted stretching?

Pilates focuses on controlled strength and stability. Mobility work explores how comfortably you move through different ranges of motion. Assisted stretching involves guided stretches with a practitioner.

Do I need to be flexible to get started?

Absolutely not! Flexibility isn't a prerequisite. Our goal is to help you feel more comfortable, capable, and confident in your movement.

What should I wear to class?

Choose comfortable clothing that lets you move freely. Any session-specific clothing or footwear recommendations will be shared ahead of time.

Do I need to bring a mat or equipment?

Equipment needs may differ by session. We'll let you know what to bring when class details are finalized.

How do I book a class?

Online reservations are planned for the website. Once booking is available, you'll be able to browse the schedule and choose a session.

Do you offer private or one-on-one sessions?

Private-session options and availability will be confirmed as our services are finalized.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our booking and cancellation policy will be shared once bookings are active.
