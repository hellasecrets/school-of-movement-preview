Classes

Explore movement offerings designed to help you build confidence in your body.

This is a general introduction to each offering. Class formats, pacing, and individual guidance may vary.

Find your foundation

Pilates

Pilates is a focused, full-body movement practice that explores strength, control, alignment, and body awareness.

What you may explore

Thoughtful, repeatable movements and attention to breath can help you notice how your body moves and build confidence over time.

Who it may suit

A welcoming starting point for people new to structured movement, as well as anyone looking for a more focused way to train.

Make space to move

Assisted Stretching

Assisted stretching is a guided movement experience that uses supportive positioning and intentional stretches to explore comfortable range.

What you may explore

With thoughtful support, you may discover different ways to move, pause, and find ease in positions that can be difficult to access alone.

Who it may suit

For anyone curious about adding supported stretching to their movement routine or seeking a slower, guided way to explore range.

Move for real life

Mobility

Mobility brings together controlled movement, range, and coordination in an approachable practice for everyday motion.

What you may explore

Mobility work can help you practice moving through different ranges with more awareness, variety, and control.

Who it may suit

Great for people who want to complement other movement practices or build a more intentional relationship with everyday movement.

Across the offerings

What to Expect

Each class has its own focus. These are a few ways guidance and movement can take shape across the schedule.

Clear guidance

Guided exercises, drills, and stretches can be paired with clear cues or communication about comfort.

Options to explore

Depending on the offering, that may include options to adjust the challenge, supportive positioning, or adaptable movement patterns.

Space to move

Thoughtful, repeatable movement, gentle transitions, and space to move with intention rather than rush.
